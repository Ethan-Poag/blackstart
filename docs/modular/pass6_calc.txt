== 2. Warm restart after a supply interruption (loose battery terminal, isolator blip, U1 UVLO/OV): C602 does not re-arm
U1 EN/UVLO has no filter: BAT_IN < ~5.2 V turns HGATE off in 3-6 us. VBAT_P (230 uF) then feeds U2 at up to 70 W: it falls ~10 V/ms
at 32 V and U2 stops near its 3.5-4 V floor within ~1-2 ms. C602 keeps U2 EN/UVLO above 1.23 V for:
  13.8 V: EN starts at 3.42 V -> held for 127-280 ms (tau 124-273 ms)
  24.0 V: EN starts at 5.95 V -> held for 196-431 ms (tau 124-273 ms)
  28.8 V: EN starts at 7.15 V -> held for 218-480 ms (tau 124-273 ms)
  32.0 V: EN starts at 7.94 V -> held for 231-509 ms (tau 124-273 ms)
so any interruption from ~1 ms up to that hold time restarts U2 the moment VBAT_P passes its floor, while Q2 is still ramping.

current limit, FLT# falls after 1.09-1.6 ms and FW C5.1 cuts the whole card (Pass 4: the slot switch tripped at 10.6-16.5 A, so it did not).

== 4. SS310B (MDD, C14281) reverse leakage hot (closes the Pass 5 open item)
Datasheet (docs/split_evidence/modular_pass6/ss310b_mdd.pdf): IR max 0.3 mA at 77 F (25 C) and 3 mA at 212 F (100 C), at the rated 100 V;
Fig. 2 typical at 32 % of VRRM and 100 C ~0.7 mA. RthJA 60 C/W. Bound used: the 100 V maximum, doubling every 10 C (Schottky rule).
  Tj  65 C (149 F): IR <=  0.27 mA, P <=    8 mW at 32 V, dTj  0.5 K, runaway factor 0.04 (stable)
  Tj  85 C (185 F): IR <=  1.06 mA, P <=   34 mW at 32 V, dTj  2.0 K, runaway factor 0.14 (stable)
  Tj 100 C (212 F): IR <=  3.00 mA, P <=   96 mW at 32 V, dTj  5.8 K, runaway factor 0.40 (stable)
  Tj 115 C (239 F): IR <=  8.49 mA, P <=  272 mW at 32 V, dTj 16.3 K, runaway factor 1.13 (UNSTABLE)
  Tj 125 C (257 F): IR <= 16.97 mA, P <=  543 mW at 32 V, dTj 32.6 K, runaway factor 2.26 (UNSTABLE)
-> stable with 2.5x margin at a 212 F (100 C) diode junction; runaway needs the junction above ~234 F (112 C). Place it away from the eFuse
   pad heat (layout note); the slot leakage (<= 3 mA) is negligible for VBAT_SLOTS.
